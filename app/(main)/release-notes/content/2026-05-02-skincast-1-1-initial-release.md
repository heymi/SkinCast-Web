---
title: "SkinCast 1.1 - Initial App Store Release"
---

SkinCast is now available on the App Store as a daily skin forecast for changing weather.

### What shipped

- **Daily skin forecast** - See how UV, humidity, wind, temperature, and air quality may affect today's skin stress.
- **Personalized skin profile** - Set your skin type and concerns so each forecast can reflect what matters for sensitive, dry, oily, combination, or balanced skin.
- **Skin stress score** - Check a clear daily score, the main weather driver, and the conditions behind it.
- **Action plan** - Get practical routine guidance for sunscreen, barrier support, hydration, and weather-aware care.
- **No account required** - Use SkinCast without sign-in, registration, or an account setup flow.
- **Pro foundation** - The first release includes the Pro tier foundation for richer analysis and extended weather-aware guidance.

### Guidance sources

SkinCast's suggestions are informed by weather data together with public guidance and standards from organizations such as the AAD, FDA, EPA, and WHO, plus dermatology research and skincare literature.

SkinCast is designed for informational and wellness use. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions.
