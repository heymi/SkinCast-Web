title: "SkinCast 1.2 - UV Skincare and Weather-Based Forecasts"
---

SkinCast 1.2 updates the app around its clearer App Store positioning: **SkinCast: UV Skincare** and **Weather-based skin forecast**.

### What's new

- **Sharper UV skincare focus** - Daily guidance now puts UV risk, sunscreen context, and weather-aware exposure more clearly at the center of the forecast.
- **Weather-based routine guidance** - UV, humidity, wind, temperature, and air quality are translated into practical care suggestions before the day reaches your skin.
- **Clearer skin stress story** - The app better highlights the main driver behind today's score, so you can understand whether UV, dryness, wind, heat, or air quality deserves attention.
- **Travel and changing-weather context** - Forecast language is tuned for sensitive skin, city changes, and days when conditions shift quickly.
- **Dermatology-informed references** - Guidance copy now more clearly reflects public standards and resources from groups such as the AAD, FDA, EPA, and WHO, along with dermatology journals and skincare research.
- **Updated Pro framing** - Pro is positioned around fuller AI analysis, more frequent refreshes, and extended weather-aware guidance.

### Still privacy-first

SkinCast still works without requiring an account. Your skin profile is used to personalize the daily forecast experience, and the product remains focused on simple, practical guidance.

SkinCast is for informational and wellness use only. For persistent symptoms, diagnosed conditions, medication questions, or treatment decisions, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
